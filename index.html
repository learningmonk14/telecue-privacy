TeleCue Prompter Privacy Policy

Effective date: 21 November 2026
App: TeleCue (TeleCue Prompter)
Android package: com.vinay.telecue

This policy explains how TeleCue handles information when you use its Android overlay teleprompter and Google Play subscription features. For privacy questions or requests, contact the TeleCue developer at spartanmind4ever@gmail.com.

Scripts and settings

TeleCue processes the script you type or paste to display and scroll it in a floating window. It saves that script and your font size, scrolling speed and opacity settings in the app's private storage so they are available when you return. TeleCue does not upload your script to a developer-operated server. It does not provide a TeleCue account or cloud script-sync service.

Camera, microphone and storage access

TeleCue does not request camera or microphone permission and does not capture, record, access or upload photos, video or audio. If you record while using the overlay, recording is handled by the separate camera or recording app you choose, under that app's permissions and privacy practices. TeleCue does not request broad access to your files or media library.

Display over other apps

With your permission, TeleCue displays your script over another app using Android's “Display over other apps” access. A foreground service and notification keep the overlay running while you use another app. TeleCue does not read the contents of the app underneath, record your screen or use an accessibility service. You can close or stop the overlay in TeleCue and revoke overlay access in Android settings. Text in the overlay may be visible to people viewing your screen or to other tools that capture your screen.

Subscriptions, Google Play Billing and network use

TeleCue uses Google Play Billing for subscription plans, purchases, purchase acknowledgement and restoring or checking access. The app receives product and offer details, purchase state, product identifiers and purchase tokens from Google Play and uses the purchase token to acknowledge a purchase with Google Play. The current app does not send these details to a developer-operated backend or save purchase tokens in its script/settings storage.

Google processes checkout and payment details; TeleCue does not receive your full card or bank-account details. Google Play and its Billing Library handle billing-related data and technical information needed to operate and diagnose the billing service. Billing requires network communication; the app includes internet and network-state permissions through its billing dependencies. Your script is not sent as part of billing requests. Google's handling of information is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Manage or cancel your subscription in Google Play subscriptions. Clearing app data or uninstalling TeleCue does not cancel a subscription or delete Google's transaction records.

Analytics, advertising and sharing

TeleCue does not include advertising, advertising-ID tracking, a separate analytics SDK or a developer-operated crash-reporting service. The developer does not sell your personal information. Scripts and settings are not sent by TeleCue to advertisers or analytics providers. Google Play billing processing and Android backup, described in this policy, are separate from the app's local teleprompter functions.

Android backup and device transfer

Android backup is enabled for TeleCue. Depending on your Android version, device, backup provider and settings, scripts and preferences may be included in system-managed cloud backups or device-to-device transfers and restored later. These copies are managed by Android and your backup provider, not a TeleCue cloud service. Check your device's backup settings and the provider's privacy policy to manage backups.

Retention, deletion and your choices

Your script and settings remain in app storage until you replace them, clear TeleCue's app storage in Android settings or uninstall the app. To remove a saved script, replace it in the editor and leave the editor so the change is saved, or clear the app's storage. Clearing storage or uninstalling removes the local app copy; it does not necessarily remove an existing system backup, which may restore data later. Manage those copies through your backup provider.

Billing records held by Google are subject to Google's retention practices and applicable requirements. TeleCue has no separate user account to delete. For privacy questions or requests concerning information held by the developer, use the contact email above.

Security

TeleCue uses Android's app-private storage for scripts and settings. It does not add its own encryption to those saved preferences. Protect your device and review its backup settings, especially before entering sensitive scripts. Google Play manages the billing connection and payment flow. No storage or transmission method can guarantee absolute security.

This privacy-policy website

This page is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes under the GitHub Privacy Statement. This page has no added analytics, forms, cookies or tracking scripts.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated when TeleCue's features or information handling change. The effective date above identifies the current version. Contact spartanmind4ever@gmail.com with questions about this policy.
